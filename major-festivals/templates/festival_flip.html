🎨 Festival Spotlight: Holi (aka Dhuleti)

📅 Timing

  • Season: Spring
  • Month: March
  • Approximate Dates: Varies based on Hindu lunar calendar

📍 Regions Celebrated

Celebrated across all of India, including:

  • North
  • West
  • South
  • East
  • Central

📖 Significance (Reason & Story)

  • Celebrate the arrival of spring, symbolizing renewal and rebirth.
  • Holi also signifies unity, friendship, and social harmony.

🌸 Celebration Customs

  • Color Play: Throwing vibrant colored powders and water balloons.
  • Music & Dance: Singing traditional folk songs, dancing, playing drums.
  • Social Gatherings: Friends, family, and communities unite in celebration, overcoming social barriers.
👚 Fun Fact

This is the only festival where you have to wear old clothes due to staining from colors!
